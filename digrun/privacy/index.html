Privacy Policy

Dig & Run: Grandpa’s Treasure · OneHelsing Studio · Last updated: 13 November 2026

Dig & Run: Grandpa’s Treasure is an offline treasure-hunt puzzle game developed by OneHelsing Studio. You read Grandpa’s notes, walk the map, dig for the chest and stay clear of the patrol. The app works without an account, needs no internet connection to play, and we do not operate any servers for it. This policy explains what information is involved when you use the app.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device only: your settings (language, sound, vibration, reduced motion), your progress (completed mysteries and stars, coins, tools, barn and bag, hired crew, map pieces, artifacts, keys, which tutorials and the intro you have seen) and your daily-treasure streak. This information never leaves your device, and we have no access to it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your system settings, deletes it permanently.

We do not collect, sell or share your personal data. We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services in this version.

Advertising

The app is free and is funded by ads served through Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your advertising ID, device and app information, your approximate location derived from your IP address, and your interactions with ads. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we ask for your consent through Google’s consent management platform before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy preferences.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Rewarded ads play only when you tap a button that says so. A full-screen ad never opens while you are digging.
  • The app does not track you across other companies’ apps and websites; on iPhone it does not ask for tracking permission.

Sharing

When you recommend the app or share a race code, the text is created on your device and handed to the app you select through your system’s share menu. We do not receive a copy. A race code only describes a map; it contains no personal information and is not sent to any server.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete. For data processed by Google for advertising, please refer to the Google policy linked above, or withdraw your consent under Settings → Ad privacy preferences.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page and update the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: onehelsingstudio@gmail.com
