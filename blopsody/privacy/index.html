Blopsody Privacy Policy

Published by OneHelsing Studio. Last updated 18 November 2026.

Blopsody is a block puzzle game in which every game you play becomes a short song. You don’t need an account to play, and we don’t run any servers for the app. This page explains, in plain terms, what happens to your information.

The short version

  • Your games, songs and settings stay on your phone. We never see them.
  • We don’t sell your data, and we never collect your name, email address or contacts.
  • If you set a new personal best in any game mode or level, finish the Daily Song or play a duel, the player name you choose and your results are shared on the leaderboards with an anonymous ID. Details are below.
  • To understand how the game is played and to fix crashes, the app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. Details are below.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to do that. Details are below.
  • Blopsody Plus and Remove ads are optional purchases made through Google Play or the App Store; Google or Apple handles the payment and we never see your card details.

What stays on your phone

The app saves your settings (such as language, sound and vibration), your statistics and best score, the songs in your album, an unfinished game so you can continue it later, and your Daily Song results and streak. All of this is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your phone’s settings, deletes it for good.

Usage statistics and crash reports

To see which parts of the game people enjoy and to fix problems, the app uses two Google services: Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They are not linked to your name or to any account.

  • Firebase Analytics records how the app is used, for example that a game started or ended (with its mode and score), that a Tour stop was won, that a song was saved or shared, that a reward was taken, or which screen was opened. It also records basic device and app information (device model, operating system, app version, language, and a country derived from your IP address) and a random app instance identifier. On Android it may also use your device’s advertising ID, if you allow it.
  • Firebase Crashlytics sends a report when the app crashes or hits a serious error: the technical details of the error, your device model, operating system, app version and a random installation identifier.
  • Where the law requires consent, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, statistics are sent without identifiers until you answer the consent window, and afterwards they follow your answer: if you don’t agree to information being stored on your device, Analytics works without its identifiers. You can change your choice at any time from the ad preferences option in the app’s settings.
  • We never send the contents of your games, your songs, your contacts or anything you type. We don’t sell this data. Google processes it for us under its own policy (policies.google.com/privacy). Statistics are kept for up to 14 months and crash reports for 90 days.

Ads

Blopsody is free and is paid for by ads from Google AdMob. To show ads and measure how they perform, Google may collect your device’s advertising ID, basic device and app information, an approximate location based on your IP address, and how you interact with ads. Google handles this data under its own privacy policy; you can read how it uses data from apps like ours at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

  • Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the ad preferences option in the app’s settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings.
  • Full-screen ads never appear while you are playing; they only come between games or stops. Banners are kept away from where you drag pieces.

Purchases

You can play Blopsody for free. If you like, you can buy Blopsody Plus (a monthly subscription) or a one-time Remove ads inside the app. These purchases are made through Google Play on Android and through the App Store on iPhone:

  • Google Play or Apple handles the payment and your payment details under its own terms and privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy, apple.com/legal/privacy). We never see your card details, name or email address.
  • The app saves on your phone which of these purchases are active, and asks Google Play or the App Store to confirm them when it opens. We don’t run a server, and no purchase information is sent to us.
  • Analytics records that the shop was opened and whether a purchase succeeded, without any payment details.
  • You can cancel the subscription at any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions, or on iPhone in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Uninstalling the app does not cancel it; “Restore purchases” brings your purchases back on a new phone signed in to the same Google account or Apple ID.

Remote settings

To tune how often ads appear and how big some in-game rewards are, the app downloads a small set of numbers from Google Firebase Remote Config, at most about twice a day. This request includes basic app information (app version, language, country) and a random Firebase installation identifier, but nothing about your games or who you are.

Leaderboards and duels

The leaderboards (the Daily Song, the best single-game score in each game mode, all-time and weekly, and a leaderboard for each level in the level-based modes) and friend duels use Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore, servers in the EU). To take part, the app signs in with an anonymous, random account; no name, email address or phone number is ever asked for. What is stored: that random ID, the player name you type yourself (3–16 characters), your Daily Song scores, your best results in each game mode and level (score, stars, moves or swaps, or time, depending on the mode), the number of moves, which mode or level it was and the time they were sent, and, for duels, which song or level was played and both players’ results. Player names are unique and are checked against a list of offensive words in the app before they are saved. Other players see only your player name and results, on the leaderboards and in duels. If you report another player’s name (by long-pressing or tapping their row on the leaderboard, or with the small flag on a duel card), we store your random ID, that player’s random ID and name, where it happened (leaderboard or duel) and the time, and that player is hidden on your device. We may rename or remove names that are offensive. There is no chat and no messaging. Nothing is sent until you set a new personal best in a game mode, win a level with a better result than before, finish a Daily Song, or send or open a duel. The first time, best results already saved on your phone may be sent once. Duel links contain only a random code. To have this data deleted, write to us with your player name, the duel link or the approximate time of your scores; uninstalling the app also leaves the old random account unused.

On iPhone

On iPhone, the app does not track you: it does not link what you do in the app with other companies’ apps or websites, and it never asks for Apple’s tracking permission. Your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used, so the ads you see are not chosen based on your activity. The home screen widget is not available on iPhone.

Permissions

The app uses internet access only to load ads and to send the usage statistics and crash reports described above, to download the remote settings and, if you choose to buy something, to talk to Google Play or the App Store. If you turn on the daily reminder, it asks for permission to send notifications. It doesn’t access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

Reminders, widget and sharing

The daily reminder is a notification scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent to us. The home screen widget shows a small picture and a line of text that the app saves on your phone. When you share a song card or a Daily Song result, it is created on your phone and passed to the app you pick. We never receive a copy.

Children

Blopsody is meant for people aged 13 and over and is not aimed at children. We don’t knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your choices

We don’t hold your real name or contact details. The player name you choose is stored only for leaderboards and duels and is deleted if you write to us (see above). We can’t trace the statistics above back to you. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data starts over with a new random identifier. For the data Google processes for ads, see Google’s policy above, or withdraw your consent from the app’s settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Email us at onehelsingstudio@gmail.com.

Изменить выбор можно в любой момент в разделе настроек рекламы в самом приложении.
  • Мы никогда не отправляем содержимое ваших партий, ваши песни, контакты или то, что вы вводите. Мы не продаём эти данные. Google обрабатывает их для нас по своей политике (policies.google.com/privacy). Статистика хранится до 14 месяцев, отчёты о сбоях — 90 дней.

Heta ku tu di modeke lîstikê de rekoreke xwe ya nû bişkînî, astekê bi encameke ji berê çêtir derbas bikî, Strana Rojê biqedînî an duelekê bişînî yan vekî, tiştek nayê şandin. Cara yekem, encamên herî baş ên ku jixwe li ser telefona te tomarkirî ne dikarin carekê werin şandin. Girêdanên duelê tenê kodeke rasthatî dihewînin. Ji bo jêbirina van daneyan, navê xwe yê lîstikê, girêdana duelê an dema nêzîkî ya skorên xwe ji me re binivîse; dema sepan were rakirin, hesabê rasthatî yê kevn êdî nayê bikaranîn.

Li ser iPhone

Li ser iPhone sepan te naşopîne: çalakiya te ya di sepanê de bi sepan an malperên şirketên din re girê nade û qet destûra şopandinê ya Apple napirse. Nasnameya reklamê ya cîhaza te (IDFA) nayê bikaranîn; ji ber vê yekê reklamên ku tu dibînî li gorî çalakiya te nayên hilbijartin. Widgeta ekrana sereke li ser iPhone tune ye.

Destûr

Sepan înternetê tenê ji bo barkirina reklaman û şandina statîstîkên bikaranînê û raporên têkçûnê yên li jor, daxistina mîhengên ji dûr ve û, heke tu bixwazî tiştekî bikirî, ji bo axaftina bi Google Play an App Store re bi kar tîne. Heke tu bîrxistina rojane vekî, ew destûra şandina agahdariyan dixwaze. Ew xwe nagihîne kontakt, wêne, kamera, mîkrofon an cihê te yê rast.

Bîrxistin, wîdget û parvekirin

Bîrxistina rojane agahdariyek e ku li ser telefona te tê plankirin; ji me re tiştek nayê şandin. Wîdgeta ekrana sereke wêneyekî biçûk û rêzeke nivîsê nîşan dide ku sepan li ser telefona te tomar dike. Dema tu qerteke stranê an encameke Strana Rojê parve dikî, ew li ser telefona te tê çêkirin û ji sepana ku tu hildibijêrî re tê şandin. Kopiyek qet nagihîje me.
