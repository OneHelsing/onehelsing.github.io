Blopsody Privacy Policy

Published by OneHelsing Studio. Last updated 20 October 2026.

Blopsody is a block puzzle game in which every game you play becomes a short song. You don’t need an account to play, and we don’t run any servers for the app. This page explains, in plain terms, what happens to your information.

The short version

  • Your games, songs and settings stay on your phone. We never see them.
  • We don’t sell your data, and we never collect your name, email address or contacts.
  • To understand how the game is played and to fix crashes, the app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. Details are below.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to do that. Details are below.

What stays on your phone

The app saves your settings (such as language, sound and vibration), your statistics and best score, the songs in your album, an unfinished game so you can continue it later, and your Daily Song results and streak. All of this is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your phone’s settings, deletes it for good.

Usage statistics and crash reports

To see which parts of the game people enjoy and to fix problems, the app uses two Google services: Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They are not linked to your name or to any account.

  • Firebase Analytics records how the app is used, for example that a game started or ended (with its mode and score), that a Tour stop was won, that a song was saved or shared, that a reward was taken, or which screen was opened. It also records basic device and app information (device model, operating system, app version, language, and a country derived from your IP address) and a random app instance identifier. On Android it may also use your device’s advertising ID, if you allow it.
  • Firebase Crashlytics sends a report when the app crashes or hits a serious error: the technical details of the error, your device model, operating system, app version and a random installation identifier.
  • Where the law requires consent, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, statistics are sent without identifiers until you answer the consent window, and afterwards they follow your answer: if you don’t agree to information being stored on your device, Analytics works without its identifiers. You can change your choice at any time from the ad preferences option in the app’s settings.
  • We never send the contents of your games, your songs, your contacts or anything you type. We don’t sell this data. Google processes it for us under its own policy (policies.google.com/privacy). Statistics are kept for up to 14 months and crash reports for 90 days.

Ads

Blopsody is free and is paid for by ads from Google AdMob. To show ads and measure how they perform, Google may collect your device’s advertising ID, basic device and app information, an approximate location based on your IP address, and how you interact with ads. Google handles this data under its own privacy policy; you can read how it uses data from apps like ours at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

  • Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the ad preferences option in the app’s settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings.
  • Ads never appear on the game screen while you are playing.

On iPhone

On iPhone, the app does not track you: it does not link what you do in the app with other companies’ apps or websites, and it never asks for Apple’s tracking permission. Your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used, so the ads you see are not chosen based on your activity. The home screen widget is not available on iPhone.

Permissions

The app uses internet access only to load ads and to send the usage statistics and crash reports described above. If you turn on the daily reminder, it asks for permission to send notifications. It doesn’t access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

Reminders, widget and sharing

The daily reminder is a notification scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent to us. The home screen widget shows a small picture and a line of text that the app saves on your phone. When you share a song card or a Daily Song result, it is created on your phone and passed to the app you pick. We never receive a copy.

Children

Blopsody is meant for people aged 13 and over and is not aimed at children. We don’t knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your choices

We don’t hold your name or contact details, and we can’t trace the statistics above back to you. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data starts over with a new random identifier. For the data Google processes for ads, see Google’s policy above, or withdraw your consent from the app’s settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Email us at onehelsingstudio@gmail.com.

Статистика использования и отчёты о сбоях

Чтобы видеть, какие части игры нравятся игрокам, и исправлять ошибки, приложение использует два сервиса Google: Firebase Analytics и Firebase Crashlytics. Они не связаны с вашим именем или какой-либо учётной записью.

  • Firebase Analytics записывает, как используется приложение: например, начало или окончание партии (с режимом и счётом), пройденную остановку «Гастролей», сохранение или отправку песни, полученную награду или открытый экран. Также записываются основные сведения об устройстве и приложении (модель устройства, операционная система, версия приложения, язык и страна, определённая по IP-адресу) и случайный идентификатор экземпляра приложения. На Android может использоваться и рекламный идентификатор устройства, если вы это разрешите.
  • Firebase Crashlytics отправляет отчёт, когда приложение аварийно завершается или сталкивается с серьёзной ошибкой: технические подробности ошибки, модель устройства, операционную систему, версию приложения и случайный идентификатор установки.

Изменить выбор можно в любой момент в разделе настроек рекламы в самом приложении.
  • Мы никогда не отправляем содержимое ваших партий, ваши песни, контакты или то, что вы вводите. Мы не продаём эти данные. Google обрабатывает их для нас по своей политике (policies.google.com/privacy). Статистика хранится до 14 месяцев, отчёты о сбоях — 90 дней.
