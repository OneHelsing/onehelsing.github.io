Privacy Policy

Dengo · OneHelsing Studio · Effective: 28 October 2026

Dengo is an app for learning Kurdish, developed by OneHelsing Studio. You listen to sentences spoken by real people, read along, and practise in short lessons. The app has no account and no sign-in, and we do not operate any servers for it. This policy explains what information is involved when you use the app.

Information stored on your device

Your learning progress, your settings, your streak and your reminder choices are stored only on your device. This information never leaves your device and we have no access to it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your system settings, deletes it permanently.

No account, no tracking

You never create an account or sign in, and we never ask for your name, e-mail address or contacts. The app does not track you. This version contains no analytics or crash-reporting tools (no Firebase), so we receive no statistics about how you use it. We do not sell any data.

Microphone (“Say it yourself”)

In the “Say it yourself” exercise you can record your own voice and compare it with the original. The app records only when you tap the record button. The recording stays on your phone in a temporary file, is never uploaded or sent anywhere, and is deleted when you leave the exercise. Microphone permission is optional: if you don’t allow it, the rest of the app works as usual.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. They are created entirely on your device: there is no push server, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. Reminders are optional, and you can turn them off in the app or in your system settings.

Reading instructions aloud

If “Read instructions aloud” is on, exercise instructions are read by your phone’s built-in text-to-speech engine, on your device. This voice engine is part of your phone’s system and may be provided by Google or Apple under their own terms.

Advertising

The free content (level A1) is supported by ads served through Google AdMob: banner ads, full-screen ads between lessons, a full-screen ad when the app opens, and rewarded ads that play only if you choose to watch them. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device identifiers (on Android, your advertising ID), your IP address and your interactions with ads, under its own policies. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

  • Where it is required, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google’s consent form (UMP) asks for your choice before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Me → Ad privacy choices.
  • If you do not give consent, only non-personalised ads are shown.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Dengo Plus removes all ads.

Dengo Plus subscription

Dengo Plus is a monthly or yearly subscription (the yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial) sold through Google Play Billing. Payment is processed entirely by Google Play; your payment details never reach OneHelsing. The app receives only the purchase status and a purchase token from Google Play, which it uses to unlock content. “Restore purchases” asks Google Play again. You can manage or cancel your subscription in Google Play. On iPhone, this version has no purchases.

Reviews and updates

The app may ask you to rate it, and may offer an update, using Google Play’s in-app review and in-app update libraries (Play Core). These are handled by Google Play; we do not receive your review through the app.

Recordings and books

The voices in the app come from open datasets: Mozilla Common Voice (CC0) and text-to-speech datasets of the University of Melbourne (CC BY 4.0). The picture books come from StoryWeaver (CC BY 4.0). This content is shipped with the app; it contains no personal data from users of the app.

On iPhone

On iPhone, the app does not track you and never asks for Apple’s tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency).

Children

Dengo is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

We hold no name, contact details or usage data about you, so there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete; everything the app stores is on your device, and you can delete it by uninstalling the app or clearing its data. If you have e-mailed us, you can ask us to delete that message. For data processed by Google for advertising, please refer to the Google policy linked above, or change your consent under Me → Ad privacy choices.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page and update the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: onehelsingstudio@gmail.com

Hesap açmanız ya da oturum açmanız hiçbir zaman gerekmez; adınızı, e-posta adresinizi ya da kişilerinizi istemeyiz. Uygulama sizi izlemez. Mikrofon (“Sen söyle”)

“Sen söyle” alıştırmasında kendi sesinizi kaydedip asıl kayıtla karşılaştırabilirsiniz. Uygulama yalnızca siz kayıt düğmesine dokunduğunuzda kayıt yapar. Kayıt telefonunuzda geçici bir dosyada kalır, hiçbir yere yüklenmez ya da gönderilmez ve alıştırmadan çıktığınızda silinir. Mikrofon izni isteğe bağlıdır: izin vermezseniz uygulamanın geri kalanı her zamanki gibi çalışır.

Bildirimler

Hatırlatmaları açarsanız uygulama cihazınızda yerel bildirimler zamanlar. Bildirimler tamamen cihazınızda oluşturulur: anlık bildirim sunucusu yoktur; bize ya da başka birine hiçbir şey gönderilmez. Hatırlatmalar isteğe bağlıdır; uygulamadan ya da sistem ayarlarınızdan kapatabilirsiniz.

Yönergelerin sesli okunması

“Yönergeleri sesli oku” açıksa alıştırma yönergeleri, telefonunuzun yerleşik metin okuma (TTS) motoru tarafından cihazınızda okunur. Bu ses motoru telefonunuzun sisteminin bir parçasıdır ve Google ya da Apple tarafından kendi koşullarıyla sağlanıyor olabilir.

Destûra mîkrofonê bi dilê te ye: heke tu destûrê nedî, beşên din ên sepanê wek her car dixebitin.
