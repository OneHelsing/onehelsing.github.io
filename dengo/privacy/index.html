Privacy Policy

Dengo · OneHelsing Studio · Effective: 28 November 2026

Dengo is an app for learning heritage languages (Kurdish, Zazaki, Laz, Georgian, Armenian and more), developed by OneHelsing Studio. You listen to sentences spoken by real people, read along, and practise in short lessons. The app has no account and no sign-in. Almost everything works on your device; only the optional online features described below talk to our server. This policy explains what information is involved when you use the app.

Information stored on your device

Your learning progress, your settings, your streak and your reminder choices are stored only on your device. This information never leaves your device and we have no access to it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your system settings, deletes it permanently.

No account, no tracking

You never create an account or sign in, and we never ask for your name, e-mail address or contacts. The app does not track you. We do not sell any data. To understand how lessons are used and to fix crashes, the app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics, as described below.

Usage statistics and crash reports

To see which parts of the app help people learn and to fix problems, the app uses two Google services: Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They are not linked to your name or to any account.

  • Firebase Analytics records how the app is used, for example which language variety you chose, that a lesson or review was started or finished (with the number of questions and correct answers), that a language pack was downloaded, or that the Dengo Plus page was opened and whether a purchase went through. It also records basic device and app information (device model, operating system, app version, language, and a country derived from your IP address) and a random app instance identifier. On Android it may also use your device’s advertising ID, if you allow it.
  • Firebase Crashlytics sends a report when the app crashes or hits a serious error: the technical details of the error, your device model, operating system, app version and a random installation identifier.
  • Where the law requires consent, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, statistics are sent without identifiers until you answer the consent window, and afterwards they follow your answer. You can change your choice at any time under Me → Ad privacy choices.
  • We never send your recordings, the words you look up, your answers or anything you write. Google processes this data for us under its own policy (policies.google.com/privacy). Statistics are kept for up to 14 months and crash reports for 90 days.

Online dictionary, AI conversation and “Write to us”

These features are optional and work only when you use them. They send your request to our server (Google Cloud Functions, operated by OneHelsing Studio), together with the language you learn, the app language and a random app identifier that is not linked to your name. The identifier is stored only in a salted, one-way form, to apply daily limits.

  • Online dictionary: if a word is not in the offline dictionary, the word you search is sent to our server, which writes the entry with an AI model (Anthropic Claude). The resulting dictionary entry (not who asked for it) is kept so others can find it too.
  • AI conversation (“Chat with AI”, “Ask Dengo”): the messages of the current chat are sent to our server and to Anthropic to create the reply. We do not store or log chat texts; only daily counters and cost totals are kept. Anthropic processes them under its commercial terms and does not use them to train its models.
  • “Write to us”: the message you send, its type (idea, bug, new language, other), the language you learn, the app language and your platform are stored in our database (Google Cloud Firestore) so we can read it. We do not ask for your name or e-mail address. Please do not include personal information in your message. Messages are deleted when they are no longer needed, at the latest after 24 months.

Microphone (“Say it yourself”)

In the “Say it yourself” exercise you can record your own voice and compare it with the original. The app records only when you tap the record button. The recording stays on your phone in a temporary file, is never uploaded or sent anywhere, and is deleted when you leave the exercise. Microphone permission is optional: if you don’t allow it, the rest of the app works as usual.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. They are created entirely on your device: there is no push server, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. Reminders are optional, and you can turn them off in the app or in your system settings.

Reading instructions aloud

If “Read instructions aloud” is on, exercise instructions are read by your phone’s built-in text-to-speech engine, on your device. This voice engine is part of your phone’s system and may be provided by Google or Apple under their own terms.

Advertising

The free content (level A1) is supported by ads served through Google AdMob: banner ads, full-screen ads between lessons, a full-screen ad when the app opens, and rewarded ads that play only if you choose to watch them. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device identifiers (on Android, your advertising ID), your IP address and your interactions with ads, under its own policies. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

  • Where it is required, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google’s consent form (UMP) asks for your choice before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Me → Ad privacy choices.
  • If you do not give consent, only non-personalised ads are shown.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Dengo Plus removes all ads.

Dengo Plus subscription

Dengo Plus is a monthly or yearly subscription (the yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial) sold through Google Play Billing. Payment is processed entirely by Google Play; your payment details never reach OneHelsing. The app receives only the purchase status and a purchase token from Google Play, which it uses to unlock content. “Restore purchases” asks Google Play again. You can manage or cancel your subscription in Google Play. On iPhone, this version has no purchases.

Reviews and updates

The app may ask you to rate it, and may offer an update, using Google Play’s in-app review and in-app update libraries (Play Core). These are handled by Google Play; we do not receive your review through the app.

Recordings and books

The voices in the app come from open datasets: Mozilla Common Voice (CC0) and text-to-speech datasets of the University of Melbourne (CC BY 4.0). The picture books come from StoryWeaver (CC BY 4.0). Many Turkish and English meanings in the Adyghe dictionary come from the Adyghe word lists of Anadil (CC BY 4.0). This content is shipped with the app; it contains no personal data from users of the app.

On iPhone

On iPhone, the app does not track you and never asks for Apple’s tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency).

Children

Dengo is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

We hold no name or contact details about you, and usage statistics and crash reports are not linked to you, so we cannot single out your data to export, correct or delete it; everything the app stores is on your device, and you can delete it by uninstalling the app or clearing its data. If you have e-mailed us, you can ask us to delete that message. For data processed by Google for advertising, please refer to the Google policy linked above, or change your consent under Me → Ad privacy choices.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page and update the date above.

Xwendina rêwerzan bi deng

Heke “Rêwerzan bi deng bixwîne” vekirî be, rêwerzên raahênanan ji aliyê motora axaftinê ya ku di telefona te de heye (text-to-speech) ve li ser cîhaza te tên xwendin. Ev motora dengî beşek ji pergala telefona te ye û dibe ku ji aliyê Google an Apple ve li gorî şertên wan bê peydakirin.

Reklam

Naveroka belaş (asta A1) bi reklamên ku bi rêya Google AdMob tên nîşandan tê piştgirîkirin: reklamên banner, reklamên tam-ekran di navbera dersan de, reklameke tam-ekran dema sepan vedibe û reklamên bi xelat ên ku tenê heke tu bixwazî temaşe bikî dilîzin. Ji bo nîşandan û pîvandina reklaman, Google dikare li gorî polîtîkayên xwe agahiyên wek nasnameyên cîhaza te (li ser Android, nasnameya te ya reklamê), navnîşana te ya IP û têkiliya te bi reklaman re kom bike û bi kar bîne. Tu dikarî li vir bixwînî ka Google agahiyên ji sepanên ku xizmetên wî bi kar tînin çawa bi kar tîne: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

  • Li cihên ku pêwîst e, wek Qada Aborî ya Ewropayê, Keyaniya Yekbûyî û Swîsreyê, berî ku reklam li gorî te bên kesanekirin forma razîbûnê ya Google (UMP) bijartina te dipirse. Tu dikarî bijartina xwe her dem di sepanê de ji Ez → Vebijarkên nepeniyê yên reklamê biguherînî.
  • Heke tu razîbûnê nedî, tenê reklamên ne-kesanekirî tên nîşandan.
  • Tu dikarî nasnameya xwe ya reklamê ji mîhengên cîhaza xwe nû bikî an jê bibî.
  • Dengo Plus hemû reklaman radike.

Abonetiya Dengo Plus

Dengo Plus abonetiyeke mehane an salane ye ku bi rêya Google Play Billing tê firotin (di plana salane de 7 roj ceribandina belaş heye). Dravdan bi temamî ji aliyê Google Play ve tê kirin; agahiyên te yên dravdanê tu carî nagihîjin OneHelsing. Ji bo vekirina naverokê, sepan ji Google Play tenê rewşa kirînê û nîşaneke kirînê (token) distîne. “Kirînan vegerîne” dîsa ji Google Play dipirse. Tu dikarî abonetiya xwe ji Google Play bi rê ve bibî an betal bikî. Li ser iPhone, di vê guhertoyê de kirîn tune ne.
