One app for the languages of your roots: Kurdish in four dialects, Georgian, Laz, Armenian, Circassian, Uyghur, Tatar, Kabyle, Albanian, Greek, Bulgarian and more, with new languages on the way. Bir kelime yaz, gerçek sesten dinle, oynayarak öğren.

An offline dictionary that you can search in English, Turkish or the language itself, short lessons that feel like play, every script from Latin and Arabic to Georgian, Armenian and Cyrillic, plus Dengo Radio, stories and scenes. No account.
