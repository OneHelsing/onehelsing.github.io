Privacy Policy

Wordsody · OneHelsing Studio · Last updated: 4 October 2026

Wordsody is a word puzzle game developed by OneHelsing Studio. You connect letters on a wheel to fill crosswords, travel through worlds, solve a Word of the Day and a Daily Puzzle, and keep the words you learn in a notebook. The app works without an account and without an internet connection, and we do not operate any servers for it. This policy explains what information is involved when you use the app.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device only: your settings (game language, interface language, the language you are learning, sound, vibration, reduce motion, tap-to-select letters, left-handed layout, colour-blind colours, theme, daily reminder and its time), your progress in each language (including a level you left halfway), your coins, your notebook and review schedule, your streak and streak freezes, your daily results and gift calendar, your postcards, achievements and statistics, the words you suggest, and which purchases you own. This information never leaves your device, and we have no access to it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your system settings, deletes everything permanently.

We do not collect, sell or share your personal data.

Advertising

The app is free and is funded by ads served through Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your advertising ID, device and app information, your approximate location derived from your IP address, and your interactions with ads. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we ask for your consent through Google’s consent management platform before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Manage ad preferences.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • No ads are shown while you are solving a level or a daily puzzle. Banners appear only on the home, journey map, notebook and statistics screens; full-screen ads appear only between levels, never in the first 15 levels and at most once every 3 levels.
  • You may choose to watch a rewarded ad for coins or an extra streak freeze. This is always optional.

In-app purchases

Coin packs, the no-ads option and the Everything pack are one-time purchases handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only receives a confirmation of which product you bought and remembers it on your device; "Restore purchases" asks the store again. Coins are added to your balance on your device.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device at the time you choose. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. You can turn it off in the app or in your system settings.

On iPhone

On iPhone, the app asks once whether it may track your activity for advertising (Apple’s tracking permission). If you allow it, Google may use your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA) to show ads that are more relevant to you. If you don’t, you still see ads, but they aren’t chosen for you, and the app works exactly the same. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Sharing and challenges

When you share a result or challenge a friend, the app creates a short text on your device (for example an emoji strip, a level code or a wordsody:// link) and hands it to the app you select through your system’s share menu. Opening a challenge link only opens that level in the app. We do not receive a copy.

Word suggestions

Words you suggest are kept on your device. If you choose to send them, the list is handed to the app you select through your share menu. If you send it to us by e-mail, we receive the words and your e-mail address and use them only to review the words for a future update and, if needed, to reply.

Children

Wordsody is a word game for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete. If you have e-mailed us, you can ask us to delete that message. For data processed by Google for advertising, please refer to the Google policy linked above, or withdraw your consent under Settings → Manage ad preferences.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page and update the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: onehelsingstudio@gmail.com

No iPhone, o aplicativo pergunta uma vez se pode rastrear sua atividade para fins de publicidade (a permissão de rastreamento da Apple). Se você permitir, o Google pode usar o identificador de publicidade do seu aparelho (IDFA) para mostrar anúncios mais adequados. Se não permitir, você continua vendo anúncios, mas eles não são escolhidos para você; o aplicativo funciona do mesmo jeito. Você pode mudar isso quando quiser em Ajustes → Privacidade e Segurança → Rastreamento.

Compartilhamento e desafios

Quando você compartilha um resultado ou desafia alguém, o aplicativo cria no seu aparelho um texto curto (por exemplo, uma faixa de emojis, um código de fase ou um link wordsody://) e o entrega ao aplicativo que você escolher pelo menu de compartilhamento do sistema. Abrir um link de desafio só abre aquela fase no aplicativo. Não recebemos uma cópia.

Sugestões de palavras

As palavras que você sugere ficam no seu aparelho. Se você decidir enviá-las, a lista é entregue ao aplicativo que você escolher no menu de compartilhamento. Se você enviar para nós por e-mail, recebemos as palavras e o seu endereço de e-mail e os usamos apenas para avaliar as palavras para uma próxima atualização e, se preciso, responder.

Crianças

O Wordsody é um jogo de palavras para o público em geral e não é voltado a crianças menores de 13 anos. Não coletamos intencionalmente dados pessoais de crianças.

Seus direitos

Como não guardamos dados pessoais seus, não há nada que possamos exportar, corrigir ou apagar. Se você nos enviou um e-mail, pode pedir que ele seja apagado. Para os dados que o Google trata para fins de publicidade, consulte a política do Google indicada acima ou retire seu consentimento em Configurações → Gerenciar preferências de anúncios.
