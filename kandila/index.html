Light a candle at the Lourdes grotto, float a diya on the Ganges at Varanasi, walk around the Kaaba, tie an omikuji at Fushimi Inari or toss a coin into a wishing fountain. Every place is drawn by hand, day and night, with candles that really flicker and burn down over 24 hours.Lourdes Mağarası'nda mum yak, Varanasi'de Ganj'a diya bırak, Kâbe'yi tavaf et, Fushimi Inari'de omikuji bağla ya da dilek çeşmesine para at. Her mekân gece ve gündüz elle çizildi; mumlar gerçekten titrer ve 24 saatte erir.

Count prayers with a tasbih, japa mala, rosary or prayer wheel; start a 41 Yasin ring with friends; keep a remembrance page with the days that matter in each tradition; talk to a gentle AI listener in Confession Corner.
