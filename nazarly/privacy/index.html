Privacy Policy

Nazarly · OneHelsing Studio · Last updated: 11 October 2026

Nazarly is a playful social game inspired by evil-eye folklore, developed by OneHelsing Studio. You add friends, send them a “nazar” with one tap, and they break it with a short ritual. Unlike our offline games, Nazarly needs an online service so that friends can reach each other. This policy explains what we store, why, for how long, and what you can do about it. The data controller is OneHelsing Studio, reachable at onehelsingstudio@gmail.com.

What we store

  • Account: an anonymous account identifier created by Firebase Authentication. We do not ask for your e-mail address, phone number or real name.
  • Profile: your username, display name, chosen tradition, charm colour and charm design. Charms are drawn by the app; there are no photos.
  • Age: only an age band (16–17 or 18+). Your birth year is checked on your device and is not stored.
  • Friends: your friends list, friend requests (sent, received, declined), muted friends and your invite code with its expiry and usage count.
  • Nazars: who sent a nazar to whom and when, the optional pre-written compliment attached to it, whether and with which ritual it was broken, whether it was a “strong” nazar, attempts stopped by your charm, the list of nazars you sent, and the sender you chose to reveal. The sender’s identity is stored separately and is shown to the recipient only when they reveal it.
  • Safety: people you block (including senders you block anonymously, without learning who they are) and reports you make.
  • Device and notifications: your push notification token(s), app language, time-zone offset, notification settings, quiet hours, pause setting and the date you last used the app.
  • Purchases and items: for each store purchase a hashed purchase token, the product, the store order number and dates; your Eye Mode subscription period, Eye credits, shields and charm designs.
  • Rewarded ads: a one-time token the app requests before a rewarded ad, and the ad network’s transaction ID, used to confirm that the ad was really watched.
  • Limits: daily counters (nazars sent, requests, reveals, reports) used to enforce fair-use limits.

Nazarly does not collect your phone number, contacts, location, photos or microphone, and there is no free-text messaging: the only things users can send each other are pre-defined, positive actions.

Who can see what

Only friends you have mutually accepted see your username, display name and charm. Someone who has your invite link or code sees the same before adding you; a username search shows a result only for an exact match. A nazar arrives anonymously; the recipient can choose to reveal who sent it (senders are told this before sending). Your nazars, friends and settings are not public.

Why we use it (legal bases)

  • To provide the game: accounts, friends, delivering nazars and notifications, rituals, purchases and items (performance of a contract; GDPR Art. 6(1)(b), KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)).
  • To keep it safe and fair: rate limits, blocking, reports, preventing abuse and fraud, verifying purchases and rewarded ads (legitimate interests; GDPR Art. 6(1)(f), KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)).
  • Personalised advertising only with your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a), KVKK Art. 5(1)).

We do not sell your personal data.

Service providers and where data is stored

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, Hosting). Our database and server functions run in the europe-west1 region (Belgium).
  • Google AdMob for ads, including rewarded-ad verification.
  • Google Play for payments and subscriptions, and the Play Install Referrer, which passes an invite code from an invite link to the app after installation.

These providers process data on our behalf under Google’s data processing terms, which include standard contractual clauses for transfers. For users in Turkey, using the service means personal data is transferred abroad to the EU; such transfers are carried out within the framework of Article 9 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK). For users in the EU, data is stored in the EU.

Advertising

Nazarly is free and is funded by ads served through Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may process your advertising ID, device and app information, an approximate location derived from your IP address and your interactions with ads, under its own policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we ask for your consent through Google’s consent management platform before personalised ads are shown; you can change your choice later in the app’s settings.
  • No ads appear on the friends grid, during rituals, on the nazar screen, during sign-up or on the shop and paywall. Rewarded ads are always optional and start only when you tap.
  • On iPhone, the app asks once for Apple’s tracking permission; ads work the same if you decline.

In-app purchases

Eye Mode (an auto-renewing subscription), Eye credits, shields, charm designs and the no-ads option are sold through Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details. Our server checks each purchase with the store and only then adds the item to your account. You can cancel a subscription at any time in your store account; the price, period and renewal terms are shown before you buy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, our server sends them through Firebase Cloud Messaging, in your app language. They never contain the sender’s name or the compliment. You can turn each type off, set quiet hours or pause receiving nazars in the app; the app works without notifications.

Invite links

Invite links (nazarly.web.app/i/…) contain only your invite code. If the app is not installed, the page sends the visitor to the store, and on Android the code is passed through the Play Install Referrer so it can be filled in after installation. The invite page sets no cookies and does not track visitors.

How long we keep data

  • Nazars that are not broken fade after 7 days; nazar records, stopped attempts and the stored sender identities are deleted after 90 days.
  • Rewarded-ad tokens are deleted after 2 days and ad transaction IDs after 30 days.
  • Accounts not used for 12 months are deleted automatically.
  • Purchase records are kept as long as needed to honour subscriptions, handle refunds and meet legal obligations.
  • Reports are kept as long as needed to review them.

Your rights and deleting your account

You can download a copy of your data in the app (Settings → Download my data) and delete your account at any time (Settings → Delete my account). Deletion is immediate and removes your profile, username, friends, nazars, items and settings; nazars you sent stay in recipients’ inboxes as from a “deleted account” and can no longer be revealed. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account. If you cannot use the app, e-mail us from any address with your username and we will delete the account. You also have the right to access, correct, object to or restrict processing, and to complain to your data protection authority (in Turkey, the KVKK Authority).

Age

Nazarly is for people aged 16 and over. The app asks for your birth year and does not let younger people use it. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 16, we delete it.

Security

All data is encrypted in transit. The app can only read your own records; every change goes through our server, which checks the rules.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page and update the date above.
